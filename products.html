Products

Three product lines, one point of view.

Everything NAVARC builds comes from the same place: the bridge wing, the pilot boat and the ladder. Here is each product in brief; each has its own page.

Bridge Behavior Intelligence™

U.S. patent pending

Know who is really out there.

Bridge Behavior Intelligence™ watches how every tracked vessel behaves, tests whether its claimed identity holds up against what the ship can observe, and issues a signed assessment that others can rely on.

  • BehaviorContinuous read on how each contact is maneuvering
  • IdentityClaimed identity checked against independent observation

02

Pilot Vector

Live at pilot-vector.com

One place for the numbers a pilot needs before the job.

Pilot Vector brings port-specific operational information into a single advisory app for harbor pilots. Set a home port once and it opens there every time, with the day's conditions laid out against the port's own channel geometry and schedule.

  • CurrentsSet, drift and heading to steer along each channel leg
  • BoardingSea state and lee course at the ladder
  • UKC and tideSquat, wave motion, tide and clearance for the transit
  • Traffic and ETAAIS ships to scale, and schedule checked against AIS
  • Coverage128 ports in 14 countries

03

Pilot Transfer Safety

Hardware in development

The most dangerous few seconds of a pilot's day.

Boarding a moving ship from a pilot boat by rope ladder has not changed in a century. NAVARC is developing sensor-based hardware that measures the motion, loads and sea conditions at that interface.

  • PlatformMotion-compensated boarding platform that senses and reduces boat-to-ladder motion
  • LadderSmart pilot ladder that senses height above the water and wave, sea and swell data
  • MoreFurther sensor-based transfer-safety systems, discussed under NDA
About Pilot Transfer Safety
