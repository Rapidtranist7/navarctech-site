01

Bridge Behavior Intelligence™

U.S. patent pending

Know who is really out there.

A vessel announces who it is over AIS. Nothing on the bridge can verify it. Bridge Behavior Intelligence™ gives watch officers, fleets and agencies a defensible read on every tracked contact: how it is behaving, how far its claimed identity can be trusted, and the evidence behind both.

Pilot Vector

One place for the numbers a pilot needs before the job.

Currents, boarding conditions, tide, under-keel clearance, traffic, channel depths and ETA checks for 128 ports, in one advisory app for harbor pilots. Set a home port once and it opens there every time.

Pilot Transfer Safety

Hardware in development

The most dangerous few seconds of a pilot's day.

About NAVARC

Founded and led by a working harbor pilot.

NAVARC Technologies™ was founded by Captain Ryan Sweeney, an active harbor pilot with two decades of deep-sea experience before pilotage. The company's work follows the same arc: a problem observed on the bridge or at the ladder, a solution engineered for the people who do that work, and the intellectual property to carry it to market.

NAVARC holds a portfolio of pending patent applications across maritime identity and behavior validation, pilot-transfer safety, and VDES data infrastructure, developed with outside patent counsel, university research partners, and commercial and government collaborators.
